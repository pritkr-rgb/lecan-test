New here? Take the sample test first

A 5-minute practice test with 8 questions — same screen, timer, webcam check, full screen, question palette, "Mark for review", written answers and auto-submit as the real test. Nothing is saved and you can take it any number of times. At the end you see your score and the correct answers.

Instructions

  • The timer starts when you click Start test and cannot be paused. The test submits automatically when time is up.
  • Answers are saved automatically. If your internet drops, reopen the link and enter the same full name and mobile number — you continue where you left.
  • Use a laptop / desktop with a webcam (Google Chrome). The test does not open on mobile phones.
  • Your webcam stays on for the whole test. Photos are taken regularly and checked automatically — a mobile phone in view, your face not visible, or another person in the room is flagged to your trainer.
  • The test runs in full screen. Leaving full screen, switching tabs or windows is recorded. Copy, paste and right-click are disabled.
  • If the camera is turned off, the test is blocked until you turn it on again — the timer keeps running.
  • Each test can be taken only once. For a re-test, contact your trainer.

Camera & system check

This test is proctored. Your webcam stays on during the whole test and photos are shared with your trainer. Sit in a well-lit place, face the camera, keep your mobile phone away and make sure nobody else is in the room.
